How it works

From your documents to a reliable answer in four steps

Nothing is guessed on the way. Each step shows what it found and why.

Add your knowledge

FAQ tables (CSV, Excel, JSON), PDFs, Word and text files, Markdown, or a web page. Large documents are cut into passages that can support an answer.

Choose where AI runs

On your own computer (recommended: a model that fits it is downloaded for you once you confirm), through your own API key (drop a .env file), or not at all.

Test it with evidence

Ask a question and see the answer type, the sources, what is supported, what is not, who wrote the answer and which steps were tried.

Use it

Ask through the dashboard or the API, or export a package that runs on your own computer with no connection to anything.

Capabilities

Built to be right before it is fluent

The parts that matter when a customer acts on what they are told.

Evidence before answers

A similar question is not an answer. The engine checks that the text really contains the price, the time, the policy that was asked for.

An honest “I don’t know”

When nothing answers, it says so. When part of a question is covered, it answers that part and names what is missing. When sources disagree, it shows both.

Search that reads meaning and words

Embeddings catch rewordings; keywords catch exact terms and codes. Blended, and measured on nine datasets, not assumed.

AI wording, checked

Changed prices, dropped conditions, flipped policies and planted links are caught, and the stored wording is used instead. You see when that happened. An exact, verified FAQ answer is simply given: no model is asked to rewrite what is already right.

Private by design

Each business sees only its own knowledge. API keys are encrypted on the server and never shown again. Documents are treated as data, never as instructions.

Works offline

The full engine runs on your own computer with no account, no key and no internet. Export it as a package and keep it improving from the questions you approve.

Your customers get an answer taken from your own words, or an honest “I don’t know”. Never a guess dressed up as an answer.

Why it is different

Similar is not the same as answered

The question “How much is a bath?” looks almost identical to “What services do you offer?”. Only one of them has a price in its answer.

A typical bot

Matches by similarity

Top match scores 0.97, so it replies with the services list. The customer asked for a price and is told about brushing and nail trims.

Knovent

Checks the answer contains what was asked

The same 0.97 changes nothing. The services answer states no price, so the reply is: “Nothing available covers the price,” with a way to reach a person.

Two ways to run it

On your computer, or hosted for your team

The same retrieval, evidence and verification code in both. The offline promise belongs to the local one.

Local mode

  • Runs on your computer, with no account
  • Needs no internet to answer, and no AI key
  • Train on your own FAQ, compare, export a package
  • One person at a keyboard; each project is its own business

Hosted mode

  • Sign in, create a business, invite your team
  • Roles: owner, admin, editor, viewer
  • Knowledge and answers kept in your database
  • Local AI first; your own provider key is optional and encrypted
Security

Your knowledge stays yours

Every business is isolated by the database itself. Keys, documents and conversations never cross between businesses, and sensitive changes are written to an audit trail nobody can edit.

Questions

Before you start

Do I need an AI key?

No. Without one, the engine answers from your knowledge by its own rules, and says “I don’t know” when it should. AI is optional, and it runs on your own computer first.

Does my data leave my computer?

Not with local AI. A hosted provider (free, paid or your own account) is used only if you turn that step on and agree to that provider’s data terms, and you can withdraw the agreement at any time.

What if my computer is too small to run a model?

The setup recommends models that fit your hardware, from very small and fast ones upward. If none fits, it says so, and you can choose a hosted option, or no AI at all.

How do I connect my own API key?

Drop your .env file or paste the key. The file is read in your browser for the one provider key you choose; nothing else in it is kept, and the key is stored encrypted and never shown again.

What files can I upload?

FAQ tables as CSV, TSV, Excel or JSON, and documents as PDF, Word, Markdown, text or HTML. Files that look like keys or passwords are refused, because they are not knowledge.

What happens when my knowledge does not answer a question?

You get a plain “I don’t know”. If part of a question is covered, that part is answered and the missing part is named. A model is never asked to fill the gap.

What does training do?

It teaches the small search model your own wording, on your computer, and shows measured results next to the model as downloaded. If training does not help, the report says so and the original model is kept.
