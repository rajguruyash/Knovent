Privacy

This page describes what the software does with data. Whoever runs a hosted Knovent adds their own legal details below.

What is stored

Your files, your questions and the answers given, and your settings. When you run Knovent on your own computer they are kept on that computer. In a hosted setup they are kept in that operator's database, separated per business, and an AI provider key is stored encrypted and is never shown again.

What leaves your computer

In local mode, nothing. If you turn on a hosted AI provider, or add your own API key, the question and the passages found for it are sent to that provider, only after you have read and agreed to that provider's disclosure. You can withdraw your agreement at any time and it stops at the next question.

Your controls

You can remove a file, which removes what was read from it. You can change or remove the AI setup at any time from the business page.
