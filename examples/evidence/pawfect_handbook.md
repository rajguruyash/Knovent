# Pawfect Grooming Studio — Operations Handbook

## Coat and skin preparation
Please tell the groomer about any known skin irritation, recent surgery, allergies, or sensitivity before the session begins. The studio does not diagnose or treat medical conditions. If a dog has open wounds, a contagious skin condition, or appears unwell, the appointment may be postponed and the owner may be asked to consult a veterinarian.

Dogs should arrive on a leash. Please bring any medication or care instructions that the groomer needs to know about, but staff cannot administer medication unless this has been explicitly agreed with the owner in advance.

## Late arrival and collection
If an owner is more than 15 minutes late, the studio may shorten the service or offer the next available appointment. The owner will be contacted before any major change to the agreed service. Dogs should be collected within 30 minutes of the completion message. A late collection fee of ₹100 applies for each additional 30-minute period, subject to staff availability.

## Safety and handling
For the safety of pets and staff, grooming may be paused if a dog shows severe distress or repeated attempts to bite. The groomer will contact the owner to agree on whether to continue with a modified service or stop the session. A muzzle is used only when necessary for immediate safety and after discussing it with the owner, wherever practical.

The studio uses separate, cleaned grooming tools for each appointment. Tables, tubs, and contact surfaces are cleaned and disinfected between dogs. Towels are washed after every use.

## Pick-up checklist
At collection, the owner should check the coat, nails, and any requested grooming areas with the staff member. Please report a concern before leaving so the team can inspect it with you. Photos may be taken for the appointment record only with the owner's permission.

## Opening schedule
The studio is open Tuesday to Sunday, 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM. It is closed on Mondays. The final appointment slot starts at 5:30 PM. Public-holiday hours may differ; customers should confirm by phone before travelling.
